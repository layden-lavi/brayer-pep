Our compounds average 99.2% purity by HPLC. We set a minimum release threshold of 98% — any batch that tests below this is destroyed and remanufactured. Purity certificates are available for every batch via our COA page.

Lyophilised (freeze-dried) peptides should be stored at −20°C (−4°F) in a sealed container protected from light and moisture. Once reconstituted with bacteriostatic water, peptides should be kept at 4°C (39°F) and used within 21–28 days. GLP-1 compounds (semaglutide, tirzepatide, etc.) are stable at 4°C for up to 90 days unopened.

Every batch is tested using High-Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC) for purity and identity, Mass Spectrometry (MS) to confirm molecular weight and structure, and Endotoxin testing (LAL assay) to verify sterility. All testing is performed by accredited third-party laboratories.
